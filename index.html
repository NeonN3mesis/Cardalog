Privacy Policy for Cardalog

Last Updated: November 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Cardalog ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information. We respect your privacy and are committed to processing your data securely and transparently.

1. Data Processing and Storage

On-Device Processing: Cardalog is a "local-first" application. All business card scanning, image recognition (OCR), and contact management occur directly on your Android device. We do not transmit your scanned images or contact details to any external server.

2. Permissions We Request

To function, Cardalog requires specific permissions:

  • Camera: Used solely to capture images of business cards. These images are processed locally and stored in your device's private storage.
  • Contacts (Read/Write): Used to save scanned business card details directly to your device's native address book. We do not harvest, upload, or share your contact list.

3. Payments and Subscriptions

We do not store or process your financial information (such as credit card numbers) directly. All transactions are handled by secure third-party processors:

  • Google Play Billing: All payments are processed securely by Google. We never see your financial data.
  • RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status and purchase history. RevenueCat creates an anonymous identifier to track your "Pro" status. RevenueCat does not receive your scanned contacts or card images.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services for app functionality:

  • Google Play Services (Billing & Distribution)
  • RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

5. Data Retention

Since we do not store your scanning data on our servers, you retain full control. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, the local history of scanned cards within the app is deleted. Contacts saved to your phone's address book remain there until you manually remove them.
